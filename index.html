Last Survivor

On foot: arrows or WASD to walk, Shift to run, E to get in a car.
Driving: arrows or WASD, B to brake, Down to reverse, G to shoot if you have a gun, Down plus a turn to slide, Shift to boost, E to get out.
F searches crates and car boots, R pours a jerrycan into a car, T patches it with scrap, Q drinks, C eats, Z sleeps in a stopped car, X talks to people, I opens your gear.
Combat: Space attacks, V sneaks. Equip weapons in your gear. Hit an unaware raider from behind for a takedown. Pinch with two fingers (or + and −) to zoom the view, and tap Reset zoom to go back. M opens the map (drag to pan, pinch or +/− to zoom, tap a place to plan a route), P pauses.
Touch: drag anywhere on the right side to move or steer; all action buttons are on the left.
